Solana Keygen

Create new Solana wallet addresses with their private keys. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Keep your private key secret. Anyone who has it controls the wallet and its funds. Save it somewhere safe before you leave this page; once it's gone it can't be recovered. For large amounts, use a hardware wallet.

Private key is in the base58 format that Phantom and Solflare accept under "Import private key".

keypair.json is the 64-number array the Solana CLI reads, e.g. solana-keygen pubkey keypair.json.
